Adobo

Filipino dish mixed with various spices such as meat, garlic, soy sauce and vinegar.

    Steps for cooking adobo

  1. Heat the Pan
  2. Clean the meat
  3. Slice them
  4. put oil in the pan
  5. put the chopped garlic
  6. put the meat after gisa
  7. put 1/2 of soy sauce and vinegar
  8. add water
  9. wait it to be cooked
  10. 20minutes... Finish product
